# Tide Calibration Project — San Isidro, Northern Samar

**Goal:** Calibrate online tide predictions against real-world shore observations in **San Isidro, Northern Samar** (`12.388° N, 124.331° E`) by logging timestamps, baseline chart predictions, and shore photos across different tide phases.

---

## Baseline Chart Settings

- **Active Baseline Chart:** Tide-Forecast — Allen, Northern Samar (`12.50° N, 124.28° E`)
- **Direct Link:** <https://www.tide-forecast.com/locations/Allen-2/tides/latest>
- **Previous Chart Used (Entry #1):** Tideschart — San Jose, Northern Samar (<https://www.tideschart.com/Philippines/Eastern-Visayas/Province-of-Northern-Samar/San-Jose/>)
- **Reason for Calibration:** Public online tide tables (including NAMRIA, Tideschart, and Tide-Forecast) do not reflect actual water levels at the San Isidro shoreline. Instead of switching between multiple charts, **Allen** is used as a single fixed reference curve to measure the empirical time/height offset from shore photos.

---

## Fixed Shore Reference Landmarks

To compare waterline changes consistently across photos:

1. **Concrete seawall & moored boats** in the foreground (tracking how high the water sits on the seawall and whether boats are floating or resting on the tidal flat).
2. **Rocky reef flat** directly past the seawall.
3. **Long exposed reef bar / shoal** visible past the single foreground coconut tree.
